Top 10 Hidden Mistakes

Module F — Line-by-Line Analysis
#1
Generic professional summaryPhrases like "results-driven professional" and "team player" are invisible to recruiters. Replace with specific value propositions.
#2
Duties-focused experience bulletsYou're listing what you were supposed to do, not what you actually achieved. Every bullet needs a result.
#3
No quantifiable achievementsZero numbers across 3 years of experience. Recruiters see this as a red flag — you either can't measure impact or didn't create any.
#4
Multi-column layout breaks ATS parsingYour two-column design looks clean visually but many ATS systems read it left-to-right, scrambling your content.
#5
Skills section lacks specificity"Communication skills" and "Leadership" are not technical skills. List tools, technologies, and certifications instead.
#6
Missing role-specific action verbsYour verbs (did, worked on, assisted) are passive and vague. Start bullets with power verbs: Engineered, Drove, Reduced, Scaled.
#7
No LinkedIn URL or portfolio link90% of recruiters check LinkedIn before interviews. Not having it signals you're not serious about your professional presence.
#8
Education section is oversizedFor candidates with 2+ years of experience, education should be 2–3 lines max. Yours takes up 25% of the resume.
#9
Projects section lacks business contextYou describe what you built but not who it served, what problem it solved, or what the outcome was.
#10
No tailoring to target roleThe resume reads as a general career document. It should be tuned to a specific role family to clear ATS filters.
