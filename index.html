Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a resume builder? +

No. Resume Truth Analyzer is a diagnostic tool — it analyzes and scores your existing resume, identifies what's working against you, and gives you a roadmap to fix it. You do the fixing, or upgrade to our rewrite services.

Will this guarantee me a job? +

No tool can guarantee a job. What we can guarantee is a clearer understanding of why your resume may be underperforming, and specific, actionable steps to improve it. Better resumes consistently lead to more interviews.

Can freshers and students use this? +

Absolutely. In fact, it's even more valuable for freshers since a weak resume at the start can set the wrong salary anchor. Our AI adjusts the analysis based on your experience level.

What file formats are supported? +

We support PDF, DOC, and DOCX formats. PDF is recommended for most accurate parsing. Files should be under 5MB.

Will my resume be private and secure? +

Yes. Your resume is processed securely, never sold or shared with third parties, and deleted from our servers after analysis is complete. We take privacy seriously.
